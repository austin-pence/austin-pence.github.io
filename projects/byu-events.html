BYU Calendar

There are a lot of great things happening at BYU, but finding them shouldn't take so much effort.

Visit BYU Calendar ↗See my other work →

The idea

My team wanted to make it easier for students to find events, see the details, and keep track of what they wanted to attend.

My part

During our design sprint, I worked with my team on user research, deciding which features mattered most, and turning our ideas into paper and digital prototypes. We used feedback to rethink things like search, filters, and the layout.

What I learned

It's tempting to jump right into making screens. Talking to people first helped us see which parts of the experience actually needed work.
