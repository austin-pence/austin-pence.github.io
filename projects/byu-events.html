BYU Events Discovery

BYU students have many events to choose from, but information is spread across different websites and calendars.

THE WORK

What I contributed

Worked with a student team to interview a potential user, align features to a persona, prioritize requirements with MoSCoW, and create paper and digital prototypes.

The result

A searchable event discovery experience with filters, event details, and a personal calendar concept.

Skills

User interviews, prioritization, wireframing, prototyping, group collaboration

Project context: This was a design sprint and prototype, not a publicly released app.
