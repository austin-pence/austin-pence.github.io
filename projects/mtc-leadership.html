I spend a lot of my week helping other people become better teachers.

What I do

At the Missionary Training Center, I supervise 13 Tagalog teachers. I observe classes, meet one-on-one with teachers, share feedback, and follow up to see what's improving.

One project I led

I organized Tagalog-Lahat-Lahat, a month-long push to use more Tagalog in our teaching. It meant getting approval, helping teachers prepare, and keeping everyone involved throughout the month.

What I've learned

Good leadership isn't just telling people what to do. It's listening, being consistent, and finding a way to help each person improve. I use those lessons in my tech projects too.
